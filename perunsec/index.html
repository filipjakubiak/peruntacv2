PERUN // PROTECTION DIVISION

Perun Sec

We provide tailored protective solutions that enable our clients to operate safely and with confidence — regardless of location, operational scale, or threat level.

Protection is not presence. It is an unseen advantage.

Effective protection should not interfere with the client's daily activities. It should reduce risk, preserve freedom of movement, and remain as unobtrusive as possible. That is why every assignment begins with a thorough understanding of the client's circumstances, environment, and actual needs.

Based on this assessment, we determine the appropriate level of protection, select suitable personnel, and develop procedures tailored to the nature of the assignment.

We do not rely on standardised solutions. We create measures proportionate to the threat — from the discreet support of a single specialist to the comprehensive protection of large-scale operations.

Protective solutionstailored to your needs

We provide support for both individual assignments and long-term engagements. The scope of protection is always adapted to the client's profile, activities, and operating environment.

01

Security Risk Management

We identify, assess, and manage security risks that may affect people, assets, operations, or reputation. Our approach combines risk analysis, preventive measures, operational planning, and practical procedures tailored to the client's environment.

02

Business Continuity

We help organisations prepare for disruption and maintain critical functions during emergencies, security incidents, or operational failures. Our support includes continuity planning, crisis procedures, key-personnel protection, and recovery strategies.

03

Operational Support and Deployment

We provide experienced personnel, logistics, coordination, and operational resources for assignments in complex, remote, or high-risk environments. Support can be delivered for individual projects, temporary deployments, or long-term operations.

04

Crisis Response

We provide rapid and coordinated support during security incidents, emergencies, evacuations, and other critical situations. Our priority is to protect people, stabilise the situation, and support the restoration of normal operations.

05

Secure Travel and Global Mobility

We support safe and efficient domestic and international travel through risk assessments, journey planning, secure transport, and local coordination. Each solution is tailored to the destination, traveller profile, itinerary, and current threat environment.

06

Executive Protection

We provide discreet and professional protection for executives, public figures, private clients, and individuals exposed to elevated risk. Our services are designed to preserve the client's safety, privacy, mobility, and ability to operate without unnecessary disruption.

07

Asset and Wealth Protection

We develop protective measures for valuable property, residences, collections, investments, and other critical personal or corporate assets. Our approach may include risk assessment, physical security, procedures, monitoring, and specialist personnel.

08

Security Consulting

We provide independent and practical advice to help organisations and private clients improve their security posture. Our recommendations focus on reducing exposure, strengthening preparedness, and implementing solutions proportionate to the identified risk.

09

Security Audits

We conduct structured assessments of physical security, personnel, procedures, technology, and infrastructure. Each audit identifies vulnerabilities, evaluates existing safeguards, and provides clear recommendations for improvement.

10

Medical Transport

We organise and coordinate medical transport with qualified clinical personnel, appropriate equipment, and continuous support throughout the journey. Services can be adapted to the patient's condition, destination, mode of transport, and level of medical supervision required.

11

Investigations

We provide discreet investigative support relating to security incidents, fraud, misconduct, internal concerns, and potential threats. Our work focuses on establishing facts, preserving confidentiality, and providing clear information for informed decision-making.

12

Counter-Surveillance

We deliver specialist counter-surveillance services designed to detect, assess, and mitigate hostile or unauthorised observation. Support may cover individuals, residences, workplaces, travel routes, events, and sensitive operations.

13

Training — HEAT and Medical

We deliver practical, scenario-based training that prepares participants to operate safely in hostile, remote, or high-risk environments. Our programmes combine threat awareness, crisis response, travel security, and emergency medical skills relevant to real operational conditions.

IOC 24/7

Perun Intelligence & Operations Centre — IOC

Our operations are supported by the Perun Intelligence & Operations Centre (IOC), a 24/7 facility providing real-time intelligence, situational awareness, and operational coordination.

The IOC monitors security threats, geopolitical developments, local incidents, environmental conditions, and travel risks, ensuring continuous awareness for field teams. Through rapid analysis and information sharing, it delivers timely updates that support decision-making, route adjustments, risk mitigation, and response coordination.

By combining intelligence and operational oversight, the IOC enhances the safety, efficiency, and adaptability of all missions, ensuring personnel operate with accurate, up-to-date information in dynamic environments.
